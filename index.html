I study public lands policy, how it is put into practice, and how it affects local communities. My work explores changing federal and state policy, collaboration and other democratic innovations in governance, creative approaches to implementation, and the economic opportunities and fiscal burdens public lands bring to rural places and their local governments. I take my research questions from the communities, practitioners and policymakers I work with, and I share answers in forms they can put to use.
